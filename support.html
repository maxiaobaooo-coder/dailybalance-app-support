关于 App

「今日生活」（Daily Balance）是马孝宝（Xiaobao Ma）开发的离线日常生活自查 App。你每天回答一份简短问卷，得到当日平衡分数和建议，并可以编辑「每日总结 / 今日回顾」。

可选的就寝回顾提醒是本地通知，默认时间为 21:30，可以调整，也可以关闭。另有用药提醒。趋势以折线图和日历查看每日分数。在「我的」中可切换皮肤：默认「朝阳暖色」，以及「米笺晨光」。App 内可选择语言。需要 iOS 17 或更高版本。

本 App 不是医疗器械，不用于诊断。每日平衡分数和建议只供个人日常生活自查参考，不能替代专业医疗意见。技术支持邮箱不提供医疗建议或急救服务。

常见问题

使用 App 需要联网或账户吗？

不需要。安装后，问卷、当日平衡分数、建议、每日回顾、趋势、皮肤和语言都在设备上离线使用。没有账户，也不需要登录。下载或更新 App、访问本支持网站以及发送邮件是另外的联网操作。

提醒没有出现怎么办？

就寝回顾提醒和用药提醒都是本地通知。若没有出现，请打开 iOS「设置」>「通知」，确认已允许「今日生活」发送通知。就寝回顾提醒可以关闭，关闭后不会再发出；它的默认时间是 21:30，也可以改成其他时间。通知不会发到开发者的服务器。

如何更改语言？

使用 App 内的语言选项。可选：跟随系统（system）、简体中文（zh-Hans）、繁體中文（zh-Hant）、English（en）、日本語（ja）、한국어（ko）、Español（es）、Français（fr）、Deutsch（de）。

如何切换皮肤？

在「我的」中切换。默认皮肤是「朝阳暖色」，另一款是「米笺晨光」。

如何编辑今日回顾？

当日的「每日总结 / 今日回顾」可以编辑。打开这一天的回顾，直接修改其中的内容即可。

如何备份，或把数据换到另一台设备？

不能通过本 App 备份或迁移。问卷回答、每日平衡分数、建议、每日回顾、提醒设置、皮肤和语言都只保存在当前设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。本 App 没有账户。删除 App 会移除这些本地数据。更换设备时，数据不会自动出现在新设备上。

如何申请退款？

退款由 Apple 处理，不能在本 App 内直接办理。请使用购买时的 Apple 账户前往 reportaproblem.apple.com 提交申请。

English

About the app

今日生活 (Daily Balance) is an offline daily-life self-check app by Xiaobao Ma (马孝宝). Each day you answer a short questionnaire, receive a daily balance score and suggestions, and can edit the daily review (每日总结 / 今日回顾).

The optional bedtime review reminder is a local notification. It defaults to 21:30, and you can change the time or turn it off. Medication reminders are also available. Trends show daily scores as a line chart and a calendar. In 我的 you can switch skins: 朝阳暖色 (the default) and 米笺晨光. The app includes a language picker. It requires iOS 17 or later.

This app is not a medical device and is not for diagnosis. The daily balance score and suggestions are only a personal daily-life self-check. They do not replace professional medical advice. The support email does not provide medical advice or emergency services.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need a network connection or an account?

No. After installation, the questionnaire, daily balance score, suggestions, daily review, trends, skins and language all work offline on the device. There is no account and no sign-in. Downloading or updating the app, visiting this support website and sending email are separate steps that need a connection.

What if reminders do not appear?

The bedtime review reminder and medication reminders are local notifications. If they do not appear, open iOS Settings > Notifications and confirm that 今日生活 is allowed to send notifications. The bedtime review reminder can be turned off; once it is off, it will not fire. Its default time is 21:30, and you can change that time. Notifications are not sent to a developer server.

How do I change the language?

Use the in-app language picker. The choices are System, Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans), Traditional Chinese (zh-Hant), English (en), Japanese (ja), Korean (ko), Spanish (es), French (fr) and German (de).

How do I switch the skin?

Switch it in 我的. The default skin is 朝阳暖色. The other skin is 米笺晨光.

How do I edit today’s review?

Today’s daily review (每日总结 / 今日回顾) can be edited. Open that day’s review and change its contents.

Can I back up my data or move it to another device?

Not through this app. Questionnaire answers, daily balance scores, suggestions, daily reviews, reminder settings, skin and language stay on the current device only. They are not uploaded to any server. There is no account. Deleting the app removes this local data. It does not appear automatically on a new device.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles refunds. They cannot be issued inside this app. Sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy it and submit a request at reportaproblem.apple.com.
