隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-11-01

中文 English

中文

本政策说明「今日生活」（Daily Balance，以下简称「本 App」）本身如何处理数据。本 App 由马孝宝（Xiaobao Ma）开发，是一款离线的日常生活自查工具：你回答简短的每日问卷，得到当日平衡分数和建议，并可以编辑「每日总结 / 今日回顾」。

无账户，无登录。无广告，无分析，无追踪。本 App 不收集数据，也不把数据发送到任何服务器。你输入和生成的内容只保存在这台设备上。通知仅为本地通知。

本 App 不是医疗器械，不用于诊断。每日平衡分数和建议只供个人日常生活自查参考。

账户与登录

本 App 不要求注册或登录，不建立用户账户，也不收集姓名、邮箱、电话或其他用于识别身份的资料。使用本 App 不必向开发者提交个人资料。

本地数据

问卷回答、每日平衡分数、建议、「每日总结 / 今日回顾」、提醒设置，以及皮肤和语言偏好，都只保存在当前设备上。本 App 不把这些内容上传、同步或发送给开发者或任何服务器。

删除本 App 会移除保存在设备上的这些数据。因为没有账户，更换设备时数据也不会随账户迁移。

通知

就寝回顾提醒和用药提醒都是设备上的本地通知，不是远程推送。就寝回顾提醒为可选功能，默认时间 21:30，可以调整或关闭。是否允许通知由 iOS 的系统权限决定。安排和显示这些通知不需要连接开发者的服务器。

广告、分析与追踪

本 App 不展示广告，不使用分析或追踪，也不出售、出租或共享用户数据。开发者没有通过本 App 建立用户数据库，因为本 App 不收集、也不外发这些数据。

主动联系技术支持

如果你自行用邮件联系开发者，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址和你写进邮件的内容，并且只用来回复这次咨询、处理问题和提供相关支持。这不是本 App 自动收集的数据。相关邮件只在处理该咨询和履行适用义务所需的范围和期间内保留。你可以用下方邮箱提出查阅、更正或删除请求。

技术支持不提供医疗建议。请不要发送病历，或你不希望开发者持有的其他敏感资料。开发者不会把支持邮件用于广告，也不会出售这些邮件。

本支持网站与第三方服务

这些静态页面不设置 Cookie，不使用分析工具、追踪脚本、外部字体或外部图片，也没有在线提交表单。

访问本网站和离线使用 App 是两件事。网站托管方（例如 GitHub Pages）、邮件服务商，以及 Apple 的系统和 App Store，可能按各自的政策处理 IP 地址、请求时间、浏览器信息等技术数据，用于提供服务、安全和运行维护。购买与退款由 Apple 处理，可在 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请，不经过本 App 或本网站。本政策不声称这些第三方绝不处理其服务器层面的技术数据。

托管方的做法见 GitHub 隐私声明。该链接只在你点击后打开，不是本网站加载的资源。

政策更新

如果本 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策和生效日期，使新版本反映更新后的实际做法。

English

This policy describes how 今日生活 (Daily Balance, the “App”) itself handles data. Xiaobao Ma (马孝宝) develops the App. It is an offline daily-life self-check: you answer a short daily questionnaire, receive a daily balance score and suggestions, and can edit the daily review (每日总结 / 今日回顾).

No account and no sign-in. No ads, analytics or tracking. The App does not collect data and does not send data to any server. What you enter and what the App generates stays on this device. Notifications are local only.

The App is not a medical device and is not for diagnosis. The daily balance score and suggestions are only a personal daily-life self-check.

Accounts and sign-in

The App does not require registration or sign-in, does not create a user account, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or other identifying details. You do not need to send personal information to the developer to use it.

Data stored on the device

Questionnaire answers, daily balance scores, suggestions, the daily review (每日总结 / 今日回顾), reminder settings, and skin and language preferences stay on the current device. The App does not upload, sync or send them to the developer or to any server.

Deleting the App removes this data from the device. There is no account, so the data is not carried to another device by signing in.

Notifications

The bedtime review reminder and medication reminders are local notifications on the device, not remote push. The bedtime review reminder is optional. It defaults to 21:30, and you can change the time or turn it off. iOS notification permission controls whether they can appear. Scheduling and showing them does not contact a developer server.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The App does not show ads, use analytics or tracking, or sell, rent or share user data. The developer does not keep a user database from the App, because the App does not collect this data or send it out.

Voluntary support email

If you choose to email the developer, the developer receives your email address and whatever you write, and uses it only to reply, fix the issue and provide related support. That is not automatic collection by the App. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You can use the address below to ask for access, correction or deletion.

Support email is not medical advice. Please do not send medical records or other sensitive information you do not want the developer to hold. The developer does not use support email for advertising and does not sell it.

This support website and third-party services

These static pages do not set cookies, use analytics, tracking scripts, external fonts or external images, or offer an online form.

Visiting this website is separate from using the App offline. The site host (for example GitHub Pages), email providers, and Apple’s system and App Store may process technical data such as IP addresses, request times and browser details under their own policies, to operate and secure their services. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, not by the App or this website. This policy does not claim that those third parties never process technical data on their servers.

For the host’s practices, see the GitHub Privacy Statement. That link opens only when you select it. This website does not load it as a resource.

Updates

If the App’s data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated to match what the App actually does.
