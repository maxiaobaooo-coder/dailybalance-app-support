马孝宝（Xiaobao Ma）开发的离线日常生活自查 App。每天回答一份简短问卷，得到当日平衡分数和建议。

Daily Balance is an offline daily-life self-check app by Xiaobao Ma (马孝宝). You answer a short questionnaire each day and receive a daily balance score and suggestions.

日常自查 / Daily self-check

安装之后即可离线使用。你可以查看并编辑「每日总结 / 今日回顾」。无需账户或登录，没有广告，数据也不会发送到任何服务器。

After installation it works offline. You can view and edit the daily review (每日总结 / 今日回顾). There is no account or sign-in, no advertising, and nothing is sent to a server.

提醒、趋势与偏好 / Reminders, trends and preferences

可选的就寝回顾提醒是本地通知，默认 21:30，可以调整时间或关闭。另有用药提醒；通知只在本机发出。趋势提供每日分数的折线图和日历视图。

An optional bedtime review reminder is a local notification. It defaults to 21:30, and you can change the time or turn it off. Medication reminders are also available. Notifications stay on the device. Trends include a line chart and a calendar view of daily scores.

在「我的」中可切换两款皮肤：默认「朝阳暖色」，以及「米笺晨光」。App 内语言可选跟随系统、简体中文、繁體中文、English、日本語、한국어、Español、Français 或 Deutsch。需要 iOS 17 或更高版本。所有数据只保存在这台设备上，删除 App 会把这些数据一并移除。

In 我的 you can switch between two skins: 朝阳暖色 (the default) and 米笺晨光. The in-app language picker offers System, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French or German. iOS 17 or later is required. All data stays on the device; deleting the app removes it.

本 App 不是医疗器械，不用于诊断。每日平衡分数和建议只供个人日常生活自查参考，不能替代专业医疗意见。

This app is not a medical device and is not for diagnosis. The daily balance score and suggestions are only a personal daily-life self-check. They do not replace professional medical advice.
